Frequently asked questions

What is Notion?

Notion is a tool that helps you organize your notes, tasks, projects, and other information in one place.

Is Notion free?

Notion offers a free plan with basic features, as well as paid plans with more advanced features.

What are notion templates?

Notion templates are pre-made page layouts for Notion, offering structures for various tasks and workflows.

How to use notion templates?

Find a template you like, duplicate it to your Notion workspace, and then customize it to your needs.

Who can benefit from using Notion?

Anyone looking to improve their productivity and organization, including businesses, non-profits, schools, and individuals.
